Esoteric programming languages---\emph{esolangs} for short---is a special type of programming languages that---as opposed to the most commonly used programming languages---do not follow the purpose of being useful for efficiently building powerful programs. Instead, they explore possibilities of going beyond well-established design principles of language design.

One of the most well-known esolangs is \bfck{}, where data on a tape of infinitely sized tape is modified using rudimentary operations expressed with single characters. The esolang \bfq{} adapts this idea from classical computing to quantum computing. In this project, we bring both ideas together and write an interpreter for Hybrid \bfq{}, where operations of both esolangs can be applied. Our interpreter is capable of both simulating the programmed quantum circuit, as well as translating it into a \qiskit{} circuit.
