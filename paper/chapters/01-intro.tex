Esoteric programming languages---\emph{esolangs} for short--- are a special type of programming languages that, as opposed to the most commonly used languages, do not follow the purpose of being useful for efficiently building powerful programs. Instead, they explore possibilities of going beyond established language design principles.~\cite{temkin-2017}

One of the most well-known esolangs is \bfck{}, where data on an infinite-sized tape is modified using rudimentary operations expressed with single characters.~\cite{temkin-2017,brainfuck-esolangs} The esolang \bfq{} adapts this idea from classical computing to quantum computing.~\cite{brainfuq-esolangs} In this project, we bring both ideas together and write an interpreter for \hb{}, where operations of both esolangs can be applied. Our interpreter can both simulate the programmed quantum circuit and translate it into a \qiskit{} circuit.

We chose this topic because it poses challenges across a wide range of fields: Quantum Computing with the implementation of a simulator, program analysis with the requirement to write a parser, and software engineering with the existence of components that can be reused elegantly for different tasks. The project also had the advantage of being one where we could have fun toying around with the result after successful implementation.
