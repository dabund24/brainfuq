Esoteric programming languages---\emph{esolangs} for short---is a special type of programming languages that---as opposed to the most commonly used languages---do not follow the purpose of being useful for efficiently building powerful programs. Instead, they explore possibilities of going beyond established language design principles.~\cite{temkin-2017}

One of the most well-known esolangs is \bfck{}, where data on a tape of infinite size tape is modified using rudimentary operations expressed with single characters.~\cite{temkin-2017,brainfuck-esolangs} The esolang \bfq{} adapts this idea from classical computing to quantum computing.~\cite{brainfuq-esolangs} In this project, we bring both ideas together and write an interpreter for \hb{}, where operations of both esolangs can be applied. Our interpreter is capable of both simulating the programmed quantum circuit, as well as translating it into a \qiskit{} circuit.
