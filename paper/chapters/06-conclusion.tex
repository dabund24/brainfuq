\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% What are your key learnings? Did your approach work? What `future work' could be relevant to the project?

In this project work, we implemented an interpreter, which can both simulate a \hb{} program, as well as translate it into \qiskit{}. In hindsight, we are really happy with how this project went. We managed to not just satisfy the main requirements of the project, but could also work on the bonus task. We learned that starting to work early in the project, and clearly defining how tasks are distributed, while still discussing our progress on a weekly basis all greatly simplify the task of successfully completing the project.

The main opportunity we envision to further improve our project is the ability to transfer measured qubits to the classical \bfck{} tape. Further extensions, like additional operations, may also be desirable.
