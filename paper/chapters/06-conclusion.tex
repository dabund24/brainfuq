\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% What are your key learnings? Did your approach work? What `future work' could be relevant to the project?

In this project work, we implemented an interpreter that can both simulate a \bfq{} program and translate it into \qiskit{}. In hindsight, we are really happy with how this project went. We not only satisfied the main requirements of the project but also managed to work on the bonus task. We learned that starting work early in the project, clearly defining how tasks are distributed, and still discussing our progress on a weekly basis, greatly simplifies the task of successfully completing the project.

The main opportunity we envision to further improve our project is the ability to transfer measured qubits to the classical \bfck{} tape. Further extensions, like additional operations, may also be desirable.
